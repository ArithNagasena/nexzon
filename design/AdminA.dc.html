Admin dashboard
Who can use it
Staff sign in with their own work address, separately from customer accounts. Five roles decide which of the twelve areas each person sees — the menu hides what they cannot reach, and typing the address directly does not get past it.

Catalogue and stock
Overview
Opens on what needs attention: revenue, orders awaiting action, cash still with couriers, deposits held on launches, items out of stock, open service cases and reviews to moderate. Switches between 7, 30 and 90 days or all time.
Products
Search all products by name, brand or code; filter by brand, category, publish status and stock level; see price, cost, margin and availability at a glance.
Create and edit
One form covering everything the shop page shows — name, brand, category, description, price and old price, cost, code, stock, low-stock alert level, supplier, publish status, colour swatches, storage options, full specifications, box contents, warranty wording and sale badge. Entries are checked before saving.
Inventory
Three views: current stock with units held, reserved and available; a movement log recording every increase and decrease with a reason and the staff member who made it; and the IMEI register that backs the customer authenticity checker.
Orders and delivery
Orders
Every order across all customers, searchable by order number, customer name, phone, email or tracking number, and filterable by stage, district and payment method.
Order detail
Full picture of one order — items, totals, discount, payment state, delivery address and time slot, gift message, serial numbers issued and any return raised — with the actions to confirm, pack, dispatch, mark delivered, cancel or refund, and a printable invoice.
Delivery & payments
A dispatch board showing load by district, a payments view for confirming bank transfers and recording cash collected from couriers, and a refunds queue.
