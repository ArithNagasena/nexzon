Customers and growth
Customers
Search by name, email, phone or NIC; filter by loyalty tier and district; sorted by lifetime spend, with accounts that need watching clearly flagged.
Customer profile
Everything about one person on one screen for a phone call — orders, addresses, points balance and history, reviews written, service cases, pre-order reservations and registered devices — plus manual points adjustments with a reason.
Pre-orders
Launch campaigns showing units reserved, deposits held and days to release, with a form to open a new launch (device, price, deposit, release date, allocation limit and bundle).
Campaign queue
The reservation list for one launch, held strictly in the order taken, with the tools to allocate arriving units down the queue, turn a reservation into an order, or refund a deposit if the launch slips.
Promotions
Discount rules with scope, schedule, usage and revenue; coupon codes with per-customer and total limits; and the home page flash-deal slots with their unit caps.
Service and moderation
Reviews
Moderation queue with reported items first, marking clearly whether the reviewer actually bought the product. Each opens in a panel to read the full text, add a shop reply, then publish or reject.
Service desk
Returns, warranty claims and trade-ins in one place, each with its own stages, each case opening to inspection and repair notes. Warranty claims open longer than ten days are marked in red.
Store setup
Content
Reorder and show or hide the twelve home page sections, schedule banners and top-bar messages, and manage FAQ answers and customer testimonials.
Settings
Shop details and VAT registration; delivery rates and transit times for all ten districts, free-delivery threshold and time slots; which payment methods are switched on; loyalty tiers and points rules; email and SMS message templates; and staff accounts with a role permission grid.
What the next phase adds

2
Payment gateway connection, turning the four checkout methods into real transactions.
3
